What's next

What can you do with this insight?

You have seen how climate coverage appears in Flemish media and how people perceive it. The next step is to use this awareness: question what you see, compare different sources, and help others recognise climate misinformation more critically.

01

Reflect on your own media habits

Think about where you get climate news from, which sources you trust, and how often you question the framing behind a story.

02

Compare coverage across sources

Use the dashboard to see which topics, channels, and narratives receive attention — and which ones may be underrepresented.

03

Share the quiz with others

Help us collect more perspectives and build a clearer picture of how people understand climate coverage and misinformation.
